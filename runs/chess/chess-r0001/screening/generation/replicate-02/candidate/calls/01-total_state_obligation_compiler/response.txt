# Closed obligation ledger

Scope is limited to the supplied public contract and champion artifact. No hidden evidence, scores, benchmark positions, evaluator files, or other runs are used.

## L0 — Canonical state

The sole authoritative position state is:

`(board[64], side_to_move, castling_rights, en_passant_target, halfmove_clock, fullmove_number, repetition_history)`

Required invariants:

- Board indices are fixed as `a1 = 0` through `h8 = 63`.
- Exactly one piece occupies each nonempty square; piece case determines color.
- Side, rights, en-passant, and clocks are preserved independently of board contents.
- Search state is transient and cannot alter the root game state.
- Repetition history is distinct from the current position and is carried through UCI replay and recursive search paths.

## L1 — Position construction

`startpos` must construct the standard initial position exactly.

Six-field FEN construction must:

- Decode all eight ranks and exactly 64 squares.
- Preserve piece identity, side to move, `KQkq` rights, raw en-passant target, halfmove clock, and fullmove number.
- Reject or deterministically handle malformed ranks, symbols, fields, and numeric values without silently fabricating state.
- Initialize repetition history with the constructed position; prior history cannot be inferred from a standalone FEN.
- Never retain state from the preceding `position` command.

Champion responsibilities: `Position.fen`, `Position.start`, `Engine.setpos`.

## L2 — UCI replay

For `position ... moves ...`:

- Construct the base position first.
- Generate legal moves from the current state for every token.
- Match exact UCI coordinate notation, including lowercase promotion suffixes `n`, `b`, `r`, and `q`.
- Apply moves strictly in order.
- Advance every state field after each move.
- Never silently skip an unmatched move or continue from a divergent position.
- Record each resulting position in repetition history.
- Leave `Engine.pos` equal to the final replayed state.

Champion responsibilities: `Engine.setpos`, `legal`, `notation`, `make`.

## L3 — Attack and pseudo-legal generation

Attack detection must correctly handle:

- Pawns with color-specific capture direction.
- Knights, kings, bishops, rooks, and queens.
- Sliding blockers and first occupied square semantics.
- Board-edge boundaries.
- Current board state after special-move effects.

Pseudo-legal generation must include:

- Pawn single pushes and unobstructed initial double pushes.
- Pawn captures and promotion captures.
- En-passant only on the exact target square with the correct opposing pawn present.
- All four promotion choices for quiet and capturing promotions.
- Knight, sliding-piece, and king moves.
- Castling only with the correct king, rook, right, empty transit squares, and unattacked origin, transit, and destination king squares.

Pseudo-legal generation must exclude friendly captures, illegal off-board moves, and actual king-capture moves.

Champion responsibilities: `attacked`, `pseudo`, `king`.

## L4 — King-safety legality

Every candidate move must be applied through the authoritative transition kernel, then checked for:

- Presence and location of the moving side’s king.
- The king not being attacked after the move.
- Discovered attacks and pinned-piece exposure.
- Correct en-passant discovered-line effects.
- Correct castling rook relocation.
- Correct promotion occupancy.
- No capture of the opposing king as a playable move.

Castling must additionally be rejected when the king starts in check or crosses an attacked square.

Legal generation must be the sole source for:

- UCI replay validation.
- Root move selection.
- Recursive search moves.
- Check-evasion detection.
- Terminal-position detection.

Champion responsibility: `legal`; it currently delegates state transition to `make`.

## L5 — Authoritative reversible transition

One transition kernel must be the only state-changing implementation. It must support exact apply/unapply for:

- Quiet moves.
- Captures.
- Double pawn pushes.
- Castling.
- En-passant.
- Quiet promotions.
- Capturing promotions.

Its reversible delta must contain:

- Every changed square and its prior contents.
- Moving piece and captured piece.
- En-passant victim square, when applicable.
- Castling rook source and destination squares, when applicable.
- Previous castling rights.
- Previous en-passant target.
- Previous halfmove and fullmove clocks.
- Previous side to move.
- Repetition-key/count changes.

Unapply must restore the complete prior state byte-for-byte, including history counts. Timeout, exception, cutoff, and normal return paths must all restore state.

All of these consumers must use this same kernel:

- `Engine.setpos` replay.
- `legal`.
- Root search.
- Alpha-beta recursion.
- Quiescence recursion.
- Any future transition or validation helper.

Champion responsibility: `make` is currently the only transition routine, but it creates copied positions and has no unmake/delta path. All callers relying on it must be treated as transition consumers.

## L6 — Castling rights

Rights use the fixed `KQkq` mapping and must obey:

- Moving either king clears both rights for that color.
- Moving a rook from its original square clears the corresponding right.
- Capturing a rook on its original square clears the corresponding right.
- Castling clears the moving side’s rights and relocates the rook.
- Rights are never newly granted by a move.
- Promotion to a rook never creates castling rights.
- Rights are restored exactly by unmake.
- FEN-provided rights are preserved, while castling still requires the actual rook and legal geometry.

Champion responsibility: rights updates occur inside `make`; castling construction occurs inside `pseudo`.

## L7 — En-passant state

The transition contract must:

- Set the target to the midpoint square only after a valid two-square pawn advance.
- Clear it after every other move.
- Remove exactly the adjacent opposing pawn during en-passant.
- Reset the halfmove clock for en-passant.
- Reject en-passant that leaves the moving king attacked.
- Restore target, victim square, and clocks exactly on unmake.
- Preserve a FEN-provided raw target without silently rewriting it.

For repetition identity, the effective en-passant component must reflect whether an actual legal en-passant capture exists; raw FEN state remains separately preserved.

Champion responsibilities: `Position.fen`, `pseudo`, `make`, and the transposition key in `search`.

## L8 — Promotion

Promotion must:

- Occur only when a pawn reaches the back rank.
- Replace the pawn with exactly one of queen, rook, bishop, or knight.
- Preserve color.
- Support both quiet and capturing promotion.
- Use exact UCI suffix encoding and decoding.
- Apply capture, en-passant, rights, and clock semantics consistently.
- Restore the original pawn and captured piece on unmake.
- Never permit promotion to king or pawn.

Champion responsibilities: `pseudo`, `make`, `notation`, and `mscore`.

## L9 — Clocks

The transition kernel must:

- Reset halfmove clock after every pawn move or capture, including en-passant and promotion.
- Increment halfmove clock after every other move.
- Increment fullmove number only after Black’s move.
- Preserve FEN clocks at construction.
- Restore both clocks exactly on unmake.
- Include halfmove state in any search terminal/draw determination.
- Avoid using fullmove number as a substitute for repetition identity.

Champion responsibility: clock updates occur in `make`; current transposition keys omit both clocks.

## L10 — Repetition state

Repetition history must:

- Begin at the constructed root position.
- Include every successfully replayed UCI position.
- Track positions by board, side, castling rights, and effective en-passant availability.
- Be pushed on apply and popped on unapply.
- Be path-local during recursive search.
- Not leak between sibling branches, searches, or `position` commands.
- Prevent transposition-table values from being reused where repetition status differs.
- Be reset or invalidated appropriately on `ucinewgame` and a new root position.

Champion responsibilities: no repetition representation exists in `Position`; `setpos` does not record history; recursive `make` does not track it; the TT key omits it.

## L11 — Terminal positions

Terminal adjudication must be performed from the complete current state:

- No legal moves plus own king attacked: checkmate.
- No legal moves plus own king safe: stalemate.
- Terminal scoring must be consistent with side-to-move perspective and mate distance.
- Terminal detection must happen at search leaves, including quiescence leaves.
- A non-checking position with no captures is not automatically stalemate; full legal mobility must be checked.
- Repetition and applicable move-count draw conditions must be recognized without allowing search cycles to continue indefinitely.
- Dead-position/insufficient-material handling must not be confused with evaluation heuristics.
- A terminal root with no legal move must return the UCI null sentinel `0000`; otherwise the returned move must belong to the root legal-move set.

Champion responsibilities: `search` checks full mobility at normal nodes; `qsearch` can return a static evaluation for a stalemate position because it only generates captures when not in check; no repetition or clock-draw adjudication exists.

## L12 — Deadline-safe search entry and exit

For every `go movetime <ms>`:

- Establish the deadline before any potentially expensive root work.
- Parse the requested time deterministically.
- Generate or retain a validated legal root move set under the same deadline.
- Maintain a deterministic legal fallback before iterative search begins.
- Check the deadline at search entry, recursive descent, move-generation loops, root loops, quiescence loops, and before output.
- Propagate timeout as a controlled search return, not as a generic failure.
- Restore all reversible state before returning from timeout or exception.
- Never rerun unbounded legal generation in the timeout fallback.
- Return exactly one `bestmove` line, flush it, and do so before the deadline.
- Return a legal root move whenever one exists; return `0000` only for a terminal root.
- Preserve the root position after search.

Champion responsibilities: `think`, `root`, `search`, `qsearch`, `check_time`, and the `go` handler. Current gaps include initial legal generation before the deadline, polling only at coarse node checkpoints, and an exception fallback that may perform another unbounded legal move generation.

## L13 — State consumers and ownership

The following champion components must be reconciled with the single-kernel contract:

- `Position.fen` / `Position.start`: construction authority.
- `Engine.setpos`: replay and root-state authority.
- `pseudo`: candidate construction, including special moves.
- `attacked` / `king`: read-only legality predicates.
- `make`: transition authority, currently without reversible deltas.
- `legal`: transition-and-king-safety consumer.
- `root`, `search`, `qsearch`: recursive transition consumers.
- `think`: deadline and root-result owner.
- `mscore`, `evaluate`, and TT lookup: read-only consumers whose keys and cached results must not omit rule-relevant state.
- `main`: protocol boundary, timeout handling, and exactly-once output owner.

No second board representation, ad hoc replay mutation, reconstructed special-move state, or independent legality transition may remain authoritative.
