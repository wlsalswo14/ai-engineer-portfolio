# Fog Chess Loop Results - 2026-06-05

Initial calibration:

- `observation_greedy`: observation-only baseline.
- `full_info_reference`: evaluator-side full-board upper bound, not a legal
  candidate protocol.

The smoke runner writes machine-readable summaries under `tmp/fog_chess_loop_*`.
Hidden-state leakage is tested by requiring two boards with different invisible
enemy queens to serialize to identical observations.

The runner also exposes `generate_fixture_suite(seed, scale)` for private
multi-structure panels. That generated suite extends the public smoke cases with
seeded white/black rook-king captures, visible material captures, and hidden
pawn-push positions.

Comparator update:

- `compare` emits baseline rows plus optional external references.
- `stockfish_full_info_reference` is intentionally full-information: the runner
  sends a full FEN to the UCI engine. Treat it as a calibration row, not a legal
  FoW candidate.
- `stockfish_fog_observation_agent` receives only observation JSONL, so it is the
  correct adapter point for a StockfishFogOfWar-style implementation.
- `html_fog_observation_agent` receives only observation JSONL, so it is the
  adapter point for a local HTML-backed FoW prototype wrapper.
- The compare path preserves the anti-cheat contract: observation-only agents do
  not receive `canonical_board`, `full_board`, hidden legal moves, full FEN, or
  private hidden markers.
- Plain Stockfish is not a FoW rules engine. Positions where the side to move can
  capture the opponent king are marked `unsupported_king_capture_variant` before
  invoking Stockfish, because standard chess engines do not play king-capture
  variants. A local Stockfish 16 smoke row scored `44.0` on the public fixtures
  with one unsupported variant case and no timeouts.
- Local HTML-backed adapter smoke:

    `illegal_count=0`, `timeout_count=0`;
  - generated fixtures (`seed=20260605`, `scale=3`):
    `html_fog_observation_agent=79.8824`, `leak_count=0`, `illegal_count=0`,
    `timeout_count=0`;
  - this matches the included observation-greedy baseline on the current fixture
